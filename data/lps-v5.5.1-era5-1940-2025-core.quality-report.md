# LPS v5.5.1 quality summary

v5.5.1 is a classification-only patch: all 1,678 physical-event IDs,
287,773 hourly centres and final-centre physics values are inherited unchanged
from v5.5. It replaces the pressure-depth isobar proxy with an actual closed
2-hPa contour count and evaluates intensity persistence on the complete hourly
trajectory.

The land-pressure route can supply depression status only when a centre is at
least 95% land, two actual closed isobars persist, and circulation wind reaches
17 kt. DD and stronger classes remain wind-classified. The 2016 INCOMPASS event
now reaches depression; Mocha's sustained storm classification is also restored.

For 2015–2025 the catalogue averages 13.82 total,
8.55 low, and 5.27
depression-or-stronger systems per year, compared with IMD means of
13.55, 8.91, and
4.64. Every reported broad category mean is within
the frozen 25% tolerance over the full period. These are ERA5-derived
IMD-equivalent labels, not official best-track classifications.
